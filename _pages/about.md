I am Hao Chen (陈昊), a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE) at Princeton University.

My research focuses on the dynamics and non-equilibrium phenomena of quantum many-body systems, encompassing both isolated and open quantum environments. You can find one of my featured works on the relaxation and thermalization of quantum systems in [Physical Review Letters](https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/dn82-y159).

Beyond my core research, I am fascinated by the historical development of mathematics and science. I believe that retracing the actual historical path of an idea is the most profound way to truly master it. I share my explorations into these subjects on my [Bilibili](https://space.bilibili.com/254120073) and [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@RealMechanism) channels.

About this website
======
In addition to serving as my academic homepage, this website is an open-access platform for sharing the educational materials I create.

These resources cover physics, mathematics, and the history of science. While they are not intended for formal academic publication, they are designed to provide the general audience with an accessible, concrete entry point into these complex fields. 

You can explore these materials in the [Notes](https://hcprince.github.io/notes/) section.
