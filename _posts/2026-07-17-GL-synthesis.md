Description
------
This manuscript provides a brief retrospect on the emergence of the normal distribution and the central limit theorem in history, highlighting the contributions of C. F. Gauss and P. -S. Laplace. For the convenience of the readers, we do not follow the exact treatments in their original works, but provide simplified versions that preserve the spirits. During the preparation of this manuscript, we also notice a few common misconceptions in some textbooks of statistics that may lead to confusion, which are pointed out and provided with correct reasoning or derivations. We hope this short note can help readers get better understanding in related subjects like error analysis and statistical inference.
