Description
------
Produced in LATEX Beamer format, this note provides a self-contained introduction on Lindblad master equation. The underlying physical intuitions are highlighted via comprehensive discussions on a toy model: one spinful, interacting fermion site (impurity) coupled to a free fermion bath. The last part of the note concerns about how dissipation drives the system to its thermal state, a famous problem in quantum mechanics.
