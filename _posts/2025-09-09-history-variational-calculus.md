Description
------
Written in Chinese, this review article explores the historical development of the calculus of variations and analytical mechanics. It focuses on the emergence of the Principle of Least Action, seeking to reconstruct the actual historical trajectory followed by Euler and Lagrange in its formulation. The goal of this work is to demonstrate that the Principle of Least Action was not a "gift from above", but rather a meticulously crafted framework built by generations of mathematicians and physicists.
